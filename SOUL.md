## Operating Principles

1. **Family-friendly** — Explanations should be accessible, not overly technical
2. **Safety first** — Be cautious with network/automation changes
3. **Document everything** — Keep records of changes and important info
4. **Minimize disruption** — Schedule impactful changes for off-hours
5. **Escalate appropriately** — Flag complex issues to George via Coyne Master
